Introduces the divergence framework: a rank-based, parameter-free method that converts high-dimensional omics profiles into binary deviation vectors relative to a learned normal-tissue baseline. This per-sample (n-of-1) representation is robust across platforms and enables personalized biomarker discovery across gene expression, methylation, and other omics modalities. *Co-first author.*
