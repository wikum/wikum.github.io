By comparing RNA-seq of tumor-infiltrating lymphocytes with paired circulating lymphocytes across renal cell, bladder, prostate cancer, and glioblastoma, and estimating immune-cold/hot status, we identified the novel immunosuppressive cytokine Meteorin-like (METRNL) in cancer. *Co-first author.*
