Postdoctoral Associate in the [Department of Pathology and Laboratory Medicine](https://pathology.weill.cornell.edu/) at Weill Cornell Medicine, New York, NY.

My research focuses on the development of efficient and robust classification and prediction methods for clinically relevant problems in oncology, using high-dimensional data across different studies, platforms, and modalities. A central theme of my work is the **anti-profile / divergence framework** — a rank-based, parameter-free approach that frames anomaly detection in omics as deviation from a learned normal-tissue baseline, enabling per-sample (n-of-1) biomarker discovery.

Current projects span machine learning pipelines for clinical flow cytometry, multi-omics oncology studies, tumor immune-microenvironment characterization, and spatial transcriptomics.
